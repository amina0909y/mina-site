A few I keep recommending

So Can You Proof that where you start doesn't decide where you end up. Someone who had been written off rebuilt her life and became a lawyer, so a detour or a bad chapter doesn't have to define what comes next.
The New Psychology of Success Changed how I see ability: skill is built through effort, not fixed, so struggling at something doesn't mean I'm bad at it.
Give and Take Made me look at how I treat people. Generosity without boundaries burns you out, but being a giver who's selective tends to build the strongest relationships over time.
Designing Your Life Taught me to treat a career as something I can prototype and test through small experiments, instead of needing the whole path worked out before I start.
Just Keep Buying Showed me that consistency matters more than timing
